Quaestor. Phase one point five a.

An autonomous agent presents a signed authorization. Scoped to an amount, a recipient, a time window.

Core verifies it, derives a fresh settlement key from a hierarchical-deterministic vault, and signs the payment. In process. The key never touches disk.

The bridge wraps the signature and forwards. It cannot read the key. It cannot replay the signature. It cannot do anything except relay.

Twelve seconds later, settlement on Base Sepolia. Public. Verifiable. Final.

This is the boundary every other agent payments stack has to ask you to compromise.

Quaestor doesn't ask.
